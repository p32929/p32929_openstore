Motivator is an app blocker for Android that also keeps you motivated: block the apps that distract you, and every time you open one you see your own goal, your photo and a short message asking if this gets you closer to it.

What makes it different: most app blockers just say no. Motivator reminds you why you are blocking the app in the first place, with your goal and photo on the block screen, so closing the app feels like your own choice.

1. **Per app: Reminders, Block or Both.** Get a reminder while you use an app, block it, or both.
2. **Daily limit** for each app, from 5 to 240 minutes.
3. **Schedules,** like work hours on weekdays or overnight, when blocked apps stay blocked.
4. **Your goal and photo** on every reminder and block screen, with rotating messages and your own.
5. **A small hurdle before you carry on:** none, wait 10 seconds, or type a sentence.
6. **Streak, time saved and a weekly chart,** to see how you are doing.
7. **Local only:** no ads, no tracking, no network. Your goal, photo and usage stay on your phone.

I made it because I wanted an app blocker that also keeps you motivated: block the apps that distract you and remind you of your goal.

It needs Android 8.0 or newer, plus Usage access and Display over other apps, which the app asks for. Protection is off until you turn it on.
