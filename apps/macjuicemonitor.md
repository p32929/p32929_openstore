MacJuiceMonitor shows the battery level of your Bluetooth devices right in the Mac menu bar. Your mouse or keyboard usually dies in the middle of work, because checking its battery means digging into System Settings. MacJuiceMonitor keeps the levels of your mouse, keyboard and headphones in the top-right corner of your screen, so you know when to charge before they run out.

I made it because my mouse or keyboard kept dying in the middle of work, and checking their battery meant digging into System Settings.

1. **All your Bluetooth batteries at a glance** in the menu bar.
2. **Shows when a device is charging,** and turns the icon red when a battery drops below the level you pick.
3. **Works best with Apple devices** like the Magic Mouse, Magic Keyboard and AirPods. Other devices work if they report their battery over Bluetooth.
4. **Light and quiet:** it uses very little of your Mac's power and stays out of the way.

Great for anyone with a wireless mouse, keyboard or AirPods on their Mac.

Download it from the download page, install and run it. Closing the window keeps it running in the menu bar.
