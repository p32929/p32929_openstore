Sh0Sh0 is a free URL shortener with click analytics. Long links look messy when you share them, and they tell you nothing about who opened them. Paste a link, get a short one with your own custom code, and see every click: where it came from and on what device. There is no limit on links and no credit card needed.

I made it because I was looking for a URL shortener I could host myself, and almost all of them needed a lot of things to self-host. So I made one that is just a client-side React app and nothing else, so it's simple and easy to set up.

1. **Short links** with your own custom codes.
2. **Click analytics** for every link: clicks, countries and cities, devices, browsers and where the click came from.
3. **A full stats dashboard** for all your links: pick a time range, see countries and cities, devices, browsers, traffic sources and an activity heatmap, and export it all as CSV.
4. **Folders** to keep your links in order, like Work, Marketing or Personal.
5. **Password-protected links** for things only some people should see.
6. **Host an HTML page** on a short link, with the same click tracking.
7. **Free,** with no limit on links.

Handy for marketers, creators, and anyone who shares links on social media or in messages.

Want to host your own copy? Sh0Sh0 needs only Netlify and Firebase to run.
