> **Made at Rich IT (Rich Information Technology).** The app used to be on Google Play. It was taken down when the account was closed, so this is the original APK.

**An Android app for people who love birds.**

BirdsLover has a lot of birds, each with a description, scientific name, picture and its tweeting sound.

## What it does

1. **Identify birds and their species** in a second.
2. **Search** by bird name, scientific name and more.
3. **Listen to birds tweeting.**
4. **Save your favourite birds** in an offline database.
5. **Only 2 MB.**

## Install

Download the APK and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source. You can turn that permission off again afterwards.
