> **Client project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on it for a client, and the product was owned and run by them.

**Tired of deciding, shopping and cooking every day? Zesty had home chefs cook your meals and bring them to your door.**

## The problem

Deciding what to eat, shopping for it and cooking it, three times a day, every day, wears you down. Takeaway and delivery apps fix the cooking but not the cost, and the delivery fees add up.

## How Zesty solved it

Zesty was a personal chef service that put your meals on autopilot. You told it what you like, and real home cooks made fresh breakfast, lunch and dinner and brought them to your door with no delivery fees.

1. **Tell it your taste.** You filled in your cuisine preferences and food requirements.
2. **A menu made for you.** The AI designed a weekly menu around your taste and matched you with the right chefs to cook it.
3. **Real home cooks,** from stay-at-home mothers to professional chefs looking for extra income, prepared the food.
4. **No delivery fees.** Fresh breakfast, lunch and dinner arrived at your door.
5. **See who cooked it.** You could see which chef cooked your meal and track what you ate.
6. **Gets better with your feedback,** across Indian, Continental, Chinese, Italian and more cuisines.
7. **No commitment.** You could skip weeks, pause or cancel anytime.
