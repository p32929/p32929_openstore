> **Client project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on it for a client, and the product was owned and run by them.

**Zesty was a home-cooked meal delivery service. Home chefs cooked fresh breakfast, lunch and dinner to your taste and brought them to your door, with no delivery fees.**

## The problem

Deciding what to eat, shopping for it and cooking it, three times a day, every day, wears you down. Takeaway and food delivery apps take the cooking away, but the food is rarely home-style, and the delivery fees add up.

## How Zesty solved it

You told Zesty what you like to eat. It planned a weekly menu for you and matched you with home cooks near you. They cooked your meals fresh every day and delivered them free.

1. **Meals made for your taste.** You picked your cuisines and food needs, and the menu was built around them.
2. **A new menu every week,** planned by AI, so you never had to decide what to eat.
3. **Real home cooking,** made by stay-at-home mothers and professional chefs.
4. **Breakfast, lunch and dinner** at your door, with no delivery fees.
5. **Know who cooked your food,** and keep track of what you ate.
6. **Many cuisines:** Indian, Continental, Chinese, Italian and more, and it got better with your feedback.
7. **No commitment.** Skip a week, pause or cancel anytime.

## Who it was for

1. Busy working people with no time to cook.
2. Students and people living alone who miss home food.
3. Families tired of planning meals every day.
