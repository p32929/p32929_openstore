> **Client project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on it for a client, and the product was owned and run by them.

Zesty was a home-cooked meal delivery service. Deciding what to eat and cooking it three times a day gets tiring, and takeaway rarely tastes like home. With Zesty, you told it what you like, it planned a weekly menu for you, and home cooks near you made your meals fresh and brought them to your door, with no delivery fees.

1. **Meals made for your taste.** Pick your cuisines and food needs, and the menu is built around them.
2. **A new menu every week,** planned by AI, so you never have to decide what to eat.
3. **Real home cooking** by stay-at-home mothers and professional chefs.
4. **Breakfast, lunch and dinner** at your door, with no delivery fees.
5. **Know who cooked your food,** and keep track of what you ate.
6. **Many cuisines,** like Indian, Continental, Chinese and Italian, and it got better with your feedback.
7. **No commitment.** Skip a week, pause or cancel anytime.

It was a good fit for busy working people, students living away from home, and families tired of planning meals every day.
