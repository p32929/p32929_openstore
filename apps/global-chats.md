Global Chats is an anonymous chat app with a room for every country, so you can talk with people in your country and around the world without making an account. Tap once to join, get a random name, and start chatting.

What makes it different: there is no email, no phone number and no sign-up. Your country is found from your internet address, private chats are end-to-end encrypted so not even the server can read them, and public messages vanish after 24 hours.

1. **One-tap anonymous start** with a random name, like HappyLynx33. Tap for a new name any time.
2. **A Global room and a room for every country,** with how many people are online in each. Your country is picked for you, and you can change it.
3. **Private chats, end-to-end encrypted:** tap anyone's message to talk privately. The keys stay on your phone.
4. **Public messages vanish after 24 hours.**
5. **Report and block:** blocked people's messages and chats are hidden, and you can unblock them in Settings.
6. **Kept friendly:** links are blocked, swear words are masked, and you can send one message every 3 seconds.
7. **No ads, no analytics,** light and dark theme, and you can delete your account and keys at any time.

I made it because I wanted a simple global chat: a room for every country, your flag from your IP, a random name, no sign-up.

Good to know: it is for adults only and asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. It is text only, and public rooms can be read by anyone who joins. Android 7 or newer, with an internet connection.
