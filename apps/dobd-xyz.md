**Dobd.xyz was a free online video downloader. Paste a link from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest or Twitter and save the video.**

## The problem

You find a video on Instagram, a reel on Facebook or a clip on TikTok, and you want to keep it. The apps only let you watch it there, and most tools that promise to save it make you install something first.

## How Dobd.xyz solved it

Copy the link, paste it, download. That was the whole idea, right in your browser.

1. **Paste the link** and save the video or picture to your device.
2. **Nothing to install.** It worked in any browser, on a phone or a computer.
3. **Six sites:** YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest and Twitter.
4. **See what's popular:** the home page showed what other people were downloading.

## Who it was for

1. Anyone who wanted to keep a video or reel to watch offline.
2. People who share clips with friends on other apps.
3. Content creators saving their own posts.
