MegaMind Meditation is a simple meditation audio player for Android that plays your own meditation, affirmation or relaxing audios back to back, so you can put together a full session and just press play. No searching for the next file in the middle of a meditation.

What makes it different: you choose how many times each audio plays, from 1 to 10, and the whole session sits on one seek bar with every repeat included. Mix pieces from different audios into one calm session and save it as a playlist.

1. **Add your own audio files,** several at once.
2. **Repeat each track** from 1 to 10 times.
3. **One seek bar for the whole session,** so you always see how long is left.
4. **Playlists:** save the queue with a name, open it and update it later. The queue is still there after you close the app.
5. **Drag to reorder,** remove tracks, or clear the queue with undo.
6. **Plays in the background,** with a media notification and lock screen, headset and Bluetooth controls.
7. **Battery helper** that opens the right settings page, so the battery saver doesn't stop your session.
8. **Light and dark,** with your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and newer.

I made it to do meditation easily. I cut pieces from different meditation audios and put them together, with playlists.

Good to know: the app comes with no audio, you add your own files. There are no accounts and it doesn't use the internet. Everything stays on your phone.
