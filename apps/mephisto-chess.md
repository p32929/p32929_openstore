Mephisto Chess is a chess app for Android and the web that plays against you and teaches you while you play. Most chess apps either beat you without saying why, or send you off to watch lessons somewhere else. Here a teacher sits next to the board during your game: it warns you about threats, gives clues, and shows you what goes wrong when you slip, so you can take the move back and try again.

What makes it different: it is the 2005 Mephisto mobile chess engine, rebuilt so it plays just like the original, with a teacher, lessons and puzzles built around it. Everything runs on your phone or in your browser. It works offline, and there are no ads and no account.

1. **Play the engine** at 9 skill levels, from easy to hard, or play a friend on the same phone.
2. **Teacher mode:** warnings about threats, clues when you are stuck, and a board preview of what your move allows.
3. **Best move hint,** move history and undo.
4. **Lessons** on openings, opening traps, tactics, checkmate patterns, endgames and strategy.
5. **Practice:** 81 puzzles from real engine games, ten at a time in random order.
6. **A rating** that goes up and down with your results.
7. **Save and load games.**
8. **Light, dark or system theme,** four board themes and two piece sets.

Great for beginners who want to get better at chess, and for anyone who wants a quick offline game.

I loved the original Mephisto game. To play it on Android I needed an emulator, where touch didn't work and I had to use the up, down, left and right keys, which was hard. So I rewrote it properly, with the original game engine, so it plays just like the original.

Download the APK for Android (about 8 MB; the arm64 file fits almost every phone, the armeabi-v7a file is for older ones), or play it right away in your browser. The APK needs Android 7.0 or newer. The source code is private, but the app is free.
