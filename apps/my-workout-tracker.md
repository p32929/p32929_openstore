My Workout Tracker is a free workout tracker and gym log for your phone. Most workout apps ask you to pay or keep pushing a subscription before you can track much. This one is simply free: add your own exercises, log your sets each day, see the days you trained on a calendar, and watch your numbers grow on a chart.

What makes it different: there is no paywall, no account, no ads and no internet needed. Your workouts are saved only on your device, and you can back them up to a file whenever you like.

1. **Your own exercises:** track anything with your own units, like reps, kg, seconds or km, grouped by category.
2. **Daily log:** as many sets per exercise as you like, for today or any past day.
3. **See if you're improving:** each number turns green when it matches or beats your last session, and red when it's lower.
4. **Calendar:** a dot marks every day you trained.
5. **Stats:** total sessions, current and best streak, sessions per week, and a chart for every unit.
6. **Helpers while you train:** the screen stays on, and an optional tick sound counts the seconds.
7. **Backup and restore** to a file, plus light and dark mode.

Great for anyone who wants a simple, free place to log home or gym workouts.

I made this app just for myself. I needed a simple workout tracker, but almost every one I found on the Google Play Store was paid or kept asking me to pay. So I made my own. I didn't build it with other people in mind, but if it helps you too, great.

Open the app, add an exercise in Exercises, then tap Log Today's Workout and save your sets. It's still being built, so new things may change.
