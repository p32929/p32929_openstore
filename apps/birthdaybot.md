> **An old app, revived.** This app was first made around 2019 and was on Google Play until the account was closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt for modern Android phones with a new design, mainly to show it again. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

BirthdayBot is a birthday wish app, made as a personal birthday surprise for one special person. It opens with "Happy Birthday" and their name popping in with confetti and balloons, then plays the birthday song while they swipe through photo wishes like a card deck.

1. **A party intro** with the name, confetti and balloons. Tap to open the wishes.
2. **15 photo wishes** to swipe through like a card deck.
3. **Balloons fly** between the cards.
4. **The birthday song** plays softly, with a music on/off button.
5. **A big final card** with a replay button.
6. **Light and dark mode.** No ads.

I was a huge fan of Tzuyu at the time, honestly a bit crazy about her, and I had just started learning Android development. It was her birthday, so I made this app for her. After that I kept reusing it. Whenever I wanted to wish someone a happy birthday, I made a new version with their name, photos and wishes and sent it to them. They always loved it.

Download the APK from the download page and open it to install.
