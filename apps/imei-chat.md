IMEI Chat is a global chat app with no sign-up, no email and no name: open it and you are in one chat room with everyone else who uses it, and you are known only by your phone.

What makes it different: there is no account at all. Every message shows the sender's phone, like "Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra", and a short device ID, like "Device 9c6d30", so you always see who is talking without anyone making a profile.

1. **One global room** with the newest 100 messages, live updates and auto-scroll.
2. **No login:** the app connects quietly in the background. There is no sign-up screen.
3. **Your phone is your name:** the brand and model of your phone and a short device ID appear on every message you send.
4. **Your messages on the right,** everyone else's on the left with their phone, device ID and time.
5. **Report:** long-press someone's message to report it.
6. **Kept friendly:** common swear words are masked, and you can send one message every 2 seconds.
7. **Light and dark theme,** with no ads and no analytics.

I made it as a simple global chat where you don't sign up and don't pick a name: you are just your phone.

Good to know: Android 10 and newer no longer let apps read a phone's IMEI, so IMEI Chat uses a device ID instead. It is made from your phone's Android ID, scrambled so the real one never leaves your phone, and it stays the same if you reinstall. Messages are public and text only. Android 7 or newer, with an internet connection.
