Notification Light is a notification LED for Android phones that no longer have one. When the screen goes off, it shows a black screen with one thin coloured line for each app that has unread notifications, so you can see at a glance who is waiting without picking up your phone.

What makes it different: instead of one blinking dot, every app gets its own colour, so you know if it's a message, an email or just an update. It only reads which app posted a notification, never what it says.

1. **One coloured line per waiting app,** over the lock screen when the screen is off.
2. **Colour and style for each app** (solid, dashed or double). The default colour comes from the app's icon.
3. **Pulse or a steady glow,** with brightness, line width, thickness, gap and position you can change.
4. **Slow drift** to protect OLED screens from burn-in.
5. **Stays dark** in a pocket, face down or during quiet hours, if you want.
6. **Optional charging and low battery lines.**
7. **Tap a line** to open its app.
8. **Easy 3-step setup** with a live preview, plus light and dark themes.

I made it because I missed the old notification LED on phones: thin coloured lines show which apps have notifications while the screen is off.

It needs Android 10 or newer. It asks for notification access and Display over other apps, and works best with battery use set to Unrestricted.
