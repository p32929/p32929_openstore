> **An old app, revived.** This app was first made around 2019 at Rich IT (Rich Information Technology) and was on Google Play until the account was closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt for modern Android phones with a new design, mainly to show it again. Some content still comes from old online sources, so a few pictures, videos or links may not work. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

**Animal Lover is a free animal encyclopedia app with 580+ animals, each with pictures, a map, facts and a video, all in one place.**

## The problem

When you or your kids want to learn about an animal, you end up on many websites: one for a picture, one for where it lives, one for facts, and YouTube for a video. Many of those pages are full of ads and need the internet every time.

## How Animal Lover solves it

Every animal gets one tidy page with everything together, and the app keeps working offline.

1. **580+ animals in 25 classes,** from mammals and birds to reptiles and fish.
2. **Search any animal** by name.
3. **Everything on one page:** a big picture, quick facts, a description, a map of where it lives, a fun fact and its conservation status.
4. **A video for each animal.**
5. **Save favourites** in their own tab.
6. **Share** an animal with friends.
7. **Works offline** after the first load. No ads.
8. **Light and dark mode.**

## Who it is for

1. Kids and students learning about animals.
2. Parents and teachers looking for a quick, ad-free animal guide.
3. Anyone who loves wildlife.

## The story

A friend of mine made this one. He didn't study computer science, but he works in software now, writing C#, and I'm really happy for him. It was a group project: I had a Google Play account, and we published whatever we could build together. Most of us hadn't studied computer science; some came from electrical and telecommunication engineering. I was teaching them how to make apps, so we kept each app as simple as possible and as nice as we could, so people would want to download it.

We don't work together any more, but somehow I made them curious about programming, and after just a few days of building they loved it. Alhamdulillah, many of them still work in software development today, even though they didn't come from that background.
