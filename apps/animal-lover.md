> **Client project.** I built this app for a client (Rich IT). It is not my personal app. It used to be on Google Play, but the client's account was closed, so the app was taken down. This is the original APK.

**Want to know more about animals?** Animal Lover has details on more than 480 wild species, each with pictures and videos.

## What it does

1. **All animals at once.** Browse the whole list, or search for the one you want.
2. **Find animals by class.** More than 20 animal classes, each with its own icon.
3. **Facts for every animal.** Classification, conservation status, food, prey, predators, features and a fun fact.
4. **Where it lives.** A map image shows where each animal is found.
5. **Videos.** Every animal has a YouTube video for more facts.
6. **Favourites.** Mark your favourite animals, such as pets, and find them again fast.
7. **Share.** Send an animal's picture, description and video link to friends.
8. **Report a bug** from inside the app.

## Good to know

The app needs the internet to load pictures and videos, and it saves what it loads to your phone. It was made for Android phones. To install it, download the APK and allow installs from this source when Android asks.
