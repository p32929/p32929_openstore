Mega LLM is one AI chat app for all your AI models: OpenAI (ChatGPT models), Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, and any OpenAI-compatible server such as OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, xAI, Together, Ollama, LM Studio or your own vLLM. You bring your own API keys and pick your own models, on your phone, your computer or in Chrome.

What makes it different: one app talks to every provider, including the AI running on your own computer or local network, and it shows the model's thinking live as it streams.

1. **Many providers in one place,** with ready-made presets for OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, xAI, Together, Ollama and LM Studio, or any custom address.
2. **Live streaming replies** with a Stop button, plus how fast each reply came (time to first word and words per second).
3. **See the model think:** its reasoning streams live, then folds away when the answer starts.
4. **Edit, regenerate and retry** messages, with an edit history.
5. **Compare two models** side by side on the same question.
6. **Images and text files** as attachments.
7. **Rich answers:** code blocks with copy and download, tables, lists and maths formulas.
8. **Chats in folders,** with search, automatic titles and usage charts.
9. **Your own local AI:** Ollama, LM Studio and vLLM over your Wi-Fi work too.
10. **Backup and restore** everything, including your API keys, in one file. No tracking, no ads.

Good to know: you need your own API key from a provider, or your own local AI server. Your keys stay on your device and are sent only to the provider you chose. The backup file contains your API keys, so keep it private. The Chrome extension can reach any server directly, including one on your own network.

## Install

1. **Android:** download the APK from the download page and open it to install (arm64-v8a for most phones).
2. **macOS:** download the macOS zip, unzip it, move Mega LLM to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time.
3. **Windows:** download the Windows zip, unzip it and run mega_llms.exe.
4. **Linux:** download the Linux tar.gz, extract it and run the mega_llms file in the bundle folder.
5. **Chrome extension:** download the extension zip and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Click its toolbar button to open it in the side panel.
