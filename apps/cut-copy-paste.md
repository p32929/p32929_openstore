Cut Copy Paste lets you paste anywhere on Android, even in apps, forms and websites that block pasting. It is a small keyboard with Switch, Copy, Cut, Paste and Delete keys, and its Paste key types your clipboard into the field letter by letter, like a keyboard would.

What makes it different: normal paste can be blocked by an app, but typing can't. Because Cut Copy Paste types the text in instead of pasting it, apps that refuse pasted text still accept it.

1. **Paste by typing:** works where paste is blocked.
2. **Copy, Cut and Delete** for the selected text.
3. **Clipboard history:** your last 30 copies while this keyboard is open, only on the phone. It is off until you turn it on, skips passwords and clips marked sensitive, and turning it off deletes it.
4. **Pinned snippets:** save text you type often, like your address or a reply, and tap it on the keyboard to type it.
5. **Switch back** to your normal keyboard in one tap.
6. **Light and dark theme,** and a simple setup screen with a field to try it.

I made it because some websites and apps block pasting, so I made a keyboard that types your clipboard in for you.

After installing, open the app and follow the two steps to turn the keyboard on and switch to it when you need it. Android 6 or newer.
