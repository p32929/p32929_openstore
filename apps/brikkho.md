> **Made at Rich IT (Rich Information Technology).** The app used to be on Google Play. It was taken down when the account was closed, so this is the original APK.

**Know your trees. Brikkho (বৃক্ষ) is made for people who love trees.**

Search and get all the information about trees, flowers, spice plants, medicine plants and woody trees you know or do not know yet, right from your phone.

## What it does

1. **Identify plants** and trees in a second.
2. **Search** by name, scientific name or type.
3. **Save your favourite trees.**
4. **Works offline,** with a database that keeps growing.
5. **Less than 2 MB.**

## Install

Download the APK and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source. You can turn that permission off again afterwards.
