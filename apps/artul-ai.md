> **Company project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on its initial stage, and the product is owned and run by its company.

Artul.ai is an AI earnings call analysis tool for investors. A stock's price takes a second to read, but knowing whether management is being straight with you means listening to hours of earnings calls. Artul.ai does the listening for you: type a ticker and get a report on the latest call, graded from A to F. It runs as a website at artul.ai, so there is nothing to install.

1. **500 yes-or-no checks on every call.**
2. **30 markers measured,** like evasion, hype, stress and hedging.
3. **Compared with 177,437 calls since 2015,** so you see what is normal and what is not. Anything out of range gets an **H** or **L** flag.
4. **One grade from A to F** for each call. Tesla's Q4 2025 call, for example, came back a **D**, with six of ten markers out of range.
5. **Free, no signup:** last quarter's report for all 9,373 companies.
6. **Fun lists:** who said "AI" the most, who stonewalled and who hedged, and lists like calls where guidance was raised.

Useful for investors who want a quick check before buying, and analysts who can't listen to every call.

*A call grade describes a conversation, not a future price. It is a check-up, not investment advice.*
