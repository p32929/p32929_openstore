> **Company project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on its initial stage, and the product is owned and run by its company.

**Artul.ai is an AI earnings call analysis tool for investors. It grades every call from A to F, so you can see if management is being straight with you.**

## The problem

A stock's price takes a second to read. Knowing whether the people running the company are being straight with you takes much longer. The clues are buried in hours of earnings calls that almost nobody has time to listen to.

## How Artul.ai solves it

**Artul.ai does the listening for you.** Type a ticker and see the latest call's report.

1. **500 yes-or-no checks on every call.**
2. **30 markers measured,** like evasion, hype, stress and hedging.
3. **Compared with 177,437 calls since 2015,** so you see what is normal and what is not. Anything out of range gets an **H** or **L** flag.
4. **One grade from A to F** for each call. Tesla's Q4 2025 call, for example, came back a **D**, with six of ten markers out of range.
5. **Free, no signup:** last quarter's report for all 9,373 companies.
6. **Fun lists:** who said "AI" the most, who stonewalled and who hedged, and ready-made lists like calls where guidance was raised.

## Who it is for

1. Stock investors who want a quick check before they buy.
2. Analysts who can't listen to every earnings call.
3. Anyone curious how honest a company sounds.

*A call grade describes a conversation, not a future price. It is a check-up, not investment advice.*
