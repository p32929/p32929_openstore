Daily Todos is a simple to-do list widget for Android for the things you do every day, like drinking water, stretching or reading. Tick them off right on your home screen, and every night the ticks clear by themselves, so tomorrow starts fresh without you resetting anything.

What makes it different: it is made for the home screen. You tick items and add new ones in the widget itself, and the daily reset just works, with no extra permissions and no server.

1. **Home screen widget:** tick items, add new ones with +, and pick which list it shows.
2. **Resets every day** at midnight, or at a time you choose for each list (4 AM, 6 AM, or never for a normal to-do list).
3. **Several lists,** each with its own reset time.
4. **Tick, add, edit, reorder and delete** items in the app too.
5. **Progress card** that shows how much of each list is done.
6. **Light and dark,** with your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and newer.

I made it for myself: a simple to-do widget for Android for the things I repeat every day, with no server.

Good to know: everything stays on your phone, nothing is sent anywhere. It needs Android 8 or newer.

## Install

Download the APK from the download page and open it to install (arm64-v8a for most phones). Then tap Add widget in the app to put it on your home screen.
