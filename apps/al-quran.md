> **Client project.** I built this app for a client. It is not my personal app.

Al Quran by Quran Touch is a Quran app for iPhone and iPad to read, listen to and memorize the Holy Quran. Instead of switching between a printed Quran, a translation and audio files, you get the Arabic text, its meaning and the recitation together in one place, even offline.

1. **Read the full Quran** in clear Uthmani Arabic script.
2. **Listen to many reciters,** including Al-Sudais and Al-Afasy.
3. **Understand what you read** with translations in many languages and tafsir.
4. **Memorize faster.** Memorization mode repeats and loops ayat for you.
5. **Bookmarks and notes** for the ayat you want to come back to.
6. **Listen offline.** Download surahs and listen anywhere.
7. **Night mode** for reading in the dark.

Good for daily reading, for students doing hifz, and for beginners and advanced learners alike.

This was a client project. One of my mentors wanted me to work with him, but I was busy. He knew I had always wanted to build a Quran app, so he used that as the bait, and it worked.
