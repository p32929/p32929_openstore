Vision Board is a vision board app for Android where you make boards of photos and words for the goals and dreams you want to keep in front of you, play them as a slideshow, and put them on your home or lock screen so you see them every day.

What makes it different: your board doesn't stay hidden inside an app. Any slide can become your wallpaper, the wallpaper can change by itself once a day, and a home-screen widget shows a random slide. No account and no cloud, everything stays on your phone.

1. **Boards of photo and text slides.** Drag to reorder, duplicate, and undo a delete.
2. **Slideshow player** with fade or slide, shuffle, loop and seconds per slide. Play one board or all of them.
3. **Wallpaper maker** with 5 shapes and 3 styles (cards, mosaic, hero), saved as an image or a slow-zoom video.
4. **Set any slide as your wallpaper,** on the home screen, lock screen or both.
5. **New wallpaper every day** from a board you pick (off by default).
6. **Home-screen widget** that shows a random slide from your boards.
7. **Backup** of everything to a zip file, and import it back.
8. **Light, dark or system theme** and 7 colour palettes.

I made it for myself and used it, but never published it, until now.

It needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Download the APK from the download page and open it to install.
