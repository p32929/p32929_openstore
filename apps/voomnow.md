**VoomNow was a free online voice recorder, like Loom for your voice. Record in the browser and share it with a link. No app to install.**

## The problem

Sometimes a quick voice message says it better than a long email or a call you have to schedule. But recording one usually means installing an app, finding the file and working out how to send it.

## How VoomNow solved it

Open the page, press record and talk. When you stop, you get a link to share with anyone.

1. **Record in your browser.** Nothing to install.
2. **Share with a link,** private and ready to send right away.
3. **Or download** the recording to your device.
4. **No recording limit,** and free.
5. **Clear sound,** with microphone settings you can adjust.
6. **See how many times** your recording was played.

## Who it was for

1. Teams sending quick voice updates instead of long emails.
2. Teachers and freelancers explaining something by voice.
3. Anyone who wants to record and share audio fast.
