**Want to send a quick voice message without installing anything? VoomNow recorded it in your browser and gave you a link.**

## The problem

Sometimes a quick voice message says it better than a long email or a call you have to schedule. But recording one usually means installing an app, finding the file and working out how to send it.

## How VoomNow solved it

VoomNow was Loom for your voice. You opened the page, recorded, and got a link to send. Nothing to install and nothing to pay.

1. **Record in the browser.** Open the page, press Start Recording, and talk.
2. **Share a link.** When you stop, you instantly get a private link to share with anyone.
3. **Or download it** straight to your device.
4. **No limits.** There was no recording limit, nothing to install and nothing to pay.
5. **Clean sound.** You could adjust your microphone settings.
6. **Play count.** You could see how many times your recording had been played.

Record, share the link, done.
