Sometimes a quick voice message says it better than a long email or a call you have to schedule. But recording one usually means installing an app, finding the file and working out how to send it.

**VoomNow was Loom for your voice.** Open the page, press Start Recording, and talk. When you stop, you instantly get a private link to share with anyone, or you can download the recording straight to your device. There was no recording limit, nothing to install and nothing to pay. You could adjust your microphone settings for a clean sound, and see how many times your recording had been played.

Record, share the link, done.
