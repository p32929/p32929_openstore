> **An old app, revived.** This app was first made around 2019 and was on Google Play until the account was closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt with modern Android (Kotlin and Jetpack Compose) and a new design, mainly to show it again. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

**Need to test an API away from your computer? The Great Postman puts a Postman-like API client on your Android phone.**

## The problem

When you build or use an API, you often need to send a quick request and see what comes back. Usually that means sitting at your computer with Postman or a terminal. Away from your desk, there is no easy way to do it.

## How The Great Postman solves it

It is a fast, lightweight REST API client for your phone. Build any request, send it, and read the answer in a clean view. If you use Postman on your computer, you will feel at home.

1. **Send any request:** GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE, HEAD and OPTIONS, over HTTP or HTTPS.
2. **Edit everything:** query params, path params, headers, body (JSON, text, form or multipart) and auth (Bearer or Basic).
3. **Read the response** as a pretty, collapsible JSON tree, as raw text, or as headers, with status, time and size. Copy, share or search it.
4. **History** of every request, searchable.
5. **Collections** to save requests, and **environments** for values like {{baseUrl}}.
6. **Import and export cURL.** Light, dark or system theme.
