> **Company project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on it at a company, and the product was owned and run by that company.

**Instant.site was an AI website builder. You described a website in plain English and got a complete, working site, with its database, on your own domain, in minutes.**

## The problem

Getting a business online usually means a designer, a developer, a database, hosting and a domain to set up, and weeks of waiting before anyone can see the result.

## How Instant.site solved it

You described what you wanted, like a dental clinic, a restaurant, a shop or a portfolio, and the AI built the whole site.

1. **Describe it** in plain English.
2. **The AI builds it:** the design, the features and the database behind it.
3. **Go live:** connect your own domain or start on a free subdomain, then launch right away or download the code and keep building.

## Who it was for

1. Small businesses that needed a website fast.
2. Freelancers and creators who wanted a portfolio or landing page.
3. People with no coding skills.
