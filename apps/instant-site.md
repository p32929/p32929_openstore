> **Company project.** This is not a personal project. I worked on it at a company, and the product was owned and run by that company.

Instant.site was an AI website builder. Getting a business online usually meant a designer, a developer, a database, hosting and weeks of waiting. With Instant.site you described the website you wanted in plain English, like a dental clinic, a restaurant, a shop or a portfolio, and the AI built the whole thing in minutes.

1. **Describe it** in plain English.
2. **The AI builds it:** the design, the features and the database behind it.
3. **Go live:** connect your own domain or start on a free subdomain, then launch right away or download the code and keep building.

It was made for small businesses that needed a website fast, and for anyone without coding skills.
