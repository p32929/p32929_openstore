Bartabahok is an AI chat app for Android where you get messages from whoever you want, like a partner, a celebrity or a group of gamers, and they reply like real people texting you. You write who each person is and how they talk, and the app sends exactly that to the AI service you choose, with nothing added of its own.

What makes it different: you are not stuck with one company's AI or its built-in characters. Bartabahok works with any OpenAI-compatible service, such as OpenRouter, OpenAI, Groq or the free Pollinations, with your own key and any model you pick. The people are whoever you describe.

1. **Create people:** a name, who they are, how they look (optional) and a first message (optional).
2. **One-to-one and group chats:** pick two or more people for a group, and they answer you and each other, by name.
3. **Replies like texting:** answers come as several short bubbles, with typing time in between.
4. **Photos both ways:** send photos from your gallery or camera (models that understand images can see them), and with pictures turned on, people can send you generated photos and get a profile picture from their appearance.
5. **Your own rules:** one instruction sent first in every chat, and extra instructions for each chat, like the scenario or setting.
6. **Messages when you go quiet:** people can text you first after a few quiet minutes while the app is open (off by default).
7. **Private:** your keys stay on the phone in its secure storage, and there are no accounts. Light and dark theme, and you can erase all data at any time.

I made Bartabahok because I wanted an app where you get messages from whoever you want, like a partner, millionaires, gamers, anything, with human-like replies, in single or group chats. The user decides who they are.

Good to know: you need your own API key for an OpenAI-compatible AI service (Pollinations works without a key, with tight limits). That service's own rules apply. The app is for adults only and asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. Pictures need a service with an image endpoint and are off by default. Android 7 or newer.
