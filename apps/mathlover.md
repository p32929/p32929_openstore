> **An old app, revived.** This app was first made around 2019 and was on Google Play until the account was closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt for modern Android phones with a new design, mainly to show it again. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

MathLover is a free, endless maths game. Doing the same sums on paper gets boring fast, and you can't easily tell which kind you're weak at. MathLover gives you endless sums with a clock on each one and four ways to play, and your stats show how you do with each sign, so you know what to practise.

1. **Four ways to play:** Classic, Time attack (60 seconds), Survival (3 lives, gets harder) and Practice.
2. **All four signs:** add, subtract, multiply and divide.
3. **Answer your way:** pick from 4 choices or type on a number pad.
4. **See your results:** right, wrong, too slow and your best combo.
5. **Know your weak spot** with stats and best scores for each sign.
6. **Play offline,** in light or dark mode.

Good for children learning their sums, students who want to be faster in exams, and anyone who likes a quick daily brain workout.
