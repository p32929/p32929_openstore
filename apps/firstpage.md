> **An old project, from 2020.** It is kept as it was. [Home](https://openstore.pfay.workers.dev/app/p32929/home) is the newer version: a browser extension with a clock, search and your links on every new tab.

FirstPage is a simple homepage for your browser with only your own links on it. Instead of the busy page your browser opens with, you get a plain grid of the sites you actually use, one click away. Add a link once and it is there every time you open the page.

What makes it different: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to install. Your links are saved only in your own browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

1. **Your own links** in a simple grid.
2. **Add a link** with the Add button: a title and a web address.
3. **Remove links** with Delete mode.
4. **Saved in your browser,** with no account.
5. **Free and open source.**

Great for anyone who opens the same few sites every day.

I made this when I had just started learning React a bit. I have always been a solution-centric person: when something bothers me, I like to build a fix for it. I kept losing my important links, like a direct link to a Slack conversation, and finding them again was costing me time. So I made one page that keeps them all in one place.

How to use it: open the page and set it as your browser's homepage or startup page in the browser settings. Then add your links with the Add button.
