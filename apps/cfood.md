DIY complete-food recipes, the kind that replace a whole day of meals, are spread across thousands of pages. Finding one that fits your budget and diet, then working out what to buy for several of them, takes hours.

**CFood puts them all in one place.** Search over 10,000 recipes, then narrow them by cost per day, by diet tags, or by what reviewers say. Each recipe shows its daily cost, how complete its nutrition is, calories, and a protein, carbs and fat bar, so you can compare them at a glance.

Open a recipe to see every vitamin and mineral, the ingredients, the steps and the reviews. Save the ones you like, or add them to your shopping list. The list adds up each ingredient across all your recipes for the days you choose, so you buy each one once, with a link to a store for every item.

Everything you save stays in your browser, and it works on both desktop and phone.
