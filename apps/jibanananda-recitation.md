> **Made at Rich IT (Rich Information Technology).** The app used to be on Google Play. It was taken down when the account was closed, so this is the original APK.

**Poems of Jibanananda Das, to read and to listen to.**

The app has more than 50 poems by Jibanananda Das, each with an audio recitation. It is simple and easy for anybody to use.

## What it does

1. **50+ poems** to read, and the list was meant to grow.
2. **50+ audio recitations** to listen to.
3. **Save your favourite poems** for later.
4. **Read and listen offline.**
5. **A short biography** of Jibanananda Das.
6. **Only 2 MB.**

Some of the poems: Banglar Mukh, Bonolata Sen, Abar Ashibo Fire, Shonkhomala, Hai Pakhi, Ondhokar and Suronjona.

## Install

Download the APK and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source. You can turn that permission off again afterwards.
