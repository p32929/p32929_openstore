> **An old app, revived.** This app was first made around 2019 at Rich IT (Rich Information Technology) and was on Google Play until the account was closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt for modern Android phones with a new design, mainly to show it again. Some content still comes from old online sources, so a few pictures, videos or links may not work. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

A Jibanananda Das poetry app for anyone who loves Bangla poetry. Read 49 of his poems and listen to the abritti (recitation) of each one right beside the text, all in one place.

1. **Read 49 poems** and search them. The poems work offline.
2. **Listen to a recitation of each poem,** with speed control, a sleep timer and auto-next. It keeps playing with the screen off.
3. **Read comfortably** with adjustable text size and line spacing.
4. **Save your favourite poems.**
5. **A short biography** of Jibanananda Das, with its own recitation.
6. **Light and dark mode.**

This was a group project. Someone else made it, and I guided them on how to build an Android app. It was published on my account.

Recitations need the internet. Download the APK from the download page and open it to install. It needs Android 7.0 or newer.
