> **An old app.** This is the 2023 version of CGPA Calculator, uploaded as it was, with no changes. The earlier Google Play version is on the same download page. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

CGPA Calculator is a free Android app for university students who want to know their CGPA fast. Working it out by hand with credits and grade points is slow, and one wrong number usually means adding everything up again. Here you type each course's credits and GPA, tap Add, and your CGPA updates right away. Made a mistake? Tap that row, fix it, and the CGPA updates. No starting over.

1. **CGPA as you type:** each course shows its credits, GPA and total, and your CGPA is always on top.
2. **Fix or remove any course:** tap a row to update or delete it.
3. **Save your results by name,** like "Spring 2026", and open them again later.
4. **Works offline,** with no account and no sign-up.
5. **Small and simple.**

Handy when results come out, when planning the grades you need next term, or when a form asks for your CGPA.

Download the APK from the download page and open it to install.
