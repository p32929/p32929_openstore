There are thousands of Asian dramas, and most sites only let you scroll through them one by one. Finding one that fits what you feel like watching can take longer than watching it.

**DramaDB lets you narrow the whole list in a few taps.** Pick a country, a genre, a status, a year or a number of episodes, press Filter, and only the matching dramas stay on the page. You can also sort the list, for example by popularity. Each drama shows its poster and name.

It works on desktop and on a phone. It is a small hobby project, with no sign-up and no ads. The whole list sits in one file, so there is no server to run.
