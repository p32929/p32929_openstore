GradeMate is a free GPA and CGPA calculator for students anywhere, so you don't have to guess which formula your school uses or work it out by hand. Pick the method your school uses, enter your grades, and get the result.

What makes it different: every calculator explains itself. Each one says what it is for, how it works and exactly how the result is calculated, so you can trust the number and check it yourself.

1. **GPA and CGPA by semester** on the US 4.0, Bangladesh university 4.00 (UGC) and India 10-point (CBCS) scales, or with your own grade points. Start from a CGPA you already have, see each term's GPA and your running CGPA, with a trend chart. Save, open and share.
2. **SSC / HSC GPA (Bangladesh, 5.00)** with the optional 4th subject, capped at 5.00, and a fail in any compulsory subject counted correctly.
3. **Percentage** across subjects and **weighted average** for coursework, exams and quizzes.
4. **UK degree classification** (First, 2:1, 2:2, Third) with an adjustable Year 2 weight.
5. **Marks to letter grade** on the US, Bangladesh university or SSC/HSC scale, with the full table.
6. **What do I need next term?** It tells you the GPA you need, and whether it is reachable.
7. **Retake simulator** and **conversions** like CBSE CGPA to percentage, VTU, percentage to US 4.0, and any scale to any scale.
8. **Offline, no account, no ads,** with light and dark theme. Your inputs are kept as drafts.

I first made a CGPA calculator for myself. GradeMate goes further, with many grading systems, and each one explains how it works.

Good to know: universities that average both attempts of a retake, UK borderline rules and some universities' own CGPA to percentage formulas are not covered. Android 7 or newer.
