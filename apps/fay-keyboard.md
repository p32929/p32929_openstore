Fay Keyboard (ফে কীবোর্ড) is a Bengali phonetic keyboard for Android: you type Bengali the way you say it in English letters, like ami for আমি or kemon acho for কেমন আছো, and it writes Bengali as you go, without lag. It types English too.

What makes it different: it has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send what you type anywhere. Suggestions come from dictionaries built into the app, learning your words is off unless you turn it on, and password fields get plain typing with no suggestions and nothing learned.

1. **Phonetic Bengali typing** in the Avro style, with a live preview in the text field. Swipe the space bar to switch between Bengali and English.
2. **Word suggestions** for Bengali and English from 30,000 words each, even when your spelling is loose (tomake gives তোমাকে).
3. **Swipe typing** for English and Bengali (off by default).
4. **Learns your words** for better suggestions and next-word predictions, only if you turn it on, stored only on the phone, and you can clear it.
5. **Long-press keys** for symbols and accents like é, ñ, € and ₹.
6. **English autocorrect** suggestions, with optional fixing when you press space.
7. **Your look:** System, Light, Dark and Black themes, six accent colours, number row, vibration and key sound options.
8. **Private fields stay private:** password and incognito fields get no suggestions and no learning.

I made Fay Keyboard as a Bengali keyboard for myself. My old version was slow and laggy, so I rebuilt it to be fast, good looking and easy to use.

After installing, open the app and follow the two steps to turn the keyboard on and switch to it. Android 6 or newer.
