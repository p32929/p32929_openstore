> **No longer available.** Thamao was an Android app from 2018, built at 6sense Bangladesh by Nasif Siddiqui, Ahsan Asim, Ratul Rahman and Fayaz Bin Salam. It was on Google Play and passed 10,000 downloads in its first 60 days, but it has since been removed and no copy of the app could be found. This page is kept to show the project.

Thamao (থামাও, "stop it") was a free anti-theft app for Android phones in Bangladesh. When a phone is stolen, the thief also gets your photos, messages and accounts, and blackmail often follows. Thamao let you find, lock or wipe a stolen phone with one SMS. And before buying a used phone, you could check if it had been reported stolen.

1. **Find your stolen phone with one SMS.** Send a code from any number to your own number. Mobile data turns on by itself and the phone's location comes back to you.
2. **Lock it.** A message with your code locks the phone, and it can't be switched off with the power button.
3. **Wipe your data.** Remove photos, messages, files and email before the thief can use them.
4. **SIM change alert.** If someone puts a new SIM in your phone, a message goes to the number you trusted.
5. **Check a used phone by IMEI** before you buy it, to see if it was reported stolen and who the real owner is.
6. **Anti-pickpocket alarm.** A loud siren if someone takes the phone from your pocket and doesn't unlock it in time, plus headphone and charging protection.
7. **In Bangla,** with guides on what to do when a phone is stolen.

It was Bangladesh's first database-based, completely free phone anti-theft service. The app's old website is saved on the Internet Archive (link above), and the screenshots are the app's own from 2018.
