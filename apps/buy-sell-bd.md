> **An old app, revived as a showcase.** This app was first made around 2019 and its online service has since closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt for modern Android phones with a new design, using sample data instead of a server, so you can see how it worked. Anything you post stays on your phone. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

**Buy Sell BD is a free buy and sell app for Bangladesh. Post an ad without signing up and let buyers call you in one tap.**

## The problem

When you want to sell something you no longer need, or buy something used, the big marketplaces ask you to register, fill in long forms and wait. Buyers then have to dig through ads from all over the country to find something near them.

## How Buy Sell BD solves it

Anyone can post an ad without an account. Buyers filter by their division and call the seller with one tap.

1. **10 categories:** mobiles, electronics, computers, cars, motorbikes, property, home, fashion, hobbies and pets.
2. **Find things near you.** Filter by division (Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi, Rangpur, Barisal, Mymensingh), new or used, and sort by newest or price.
3. **Search** in English or Bangla.
4. **Contact the seller in one tap.** Call, SMS, share or save any ad.
5. **Post an ad with no signup,** with up to 5 photos. Edit it, mark it sold or delete it.
6. **Light and dark mode.** No ads.

## Who it is for

1. Anyone in Bangladesh selling a used phone, bike or other item.
2. Buyers looking for a good used deal nearby.

## The story

This was a group project, built with friends. I had a Google Play account, and we published whatever we could build together. Most of my friends hadn't studied computer science; some came from electrical and telecommunication engineering. I was teaching them how to make apps, so we kept each app as simple as possible and as nice as we could, so people would want to download it.

We don't work together any more, but I'm proud of what came out of it. Somehow I made them curious about programming, and after just a few days of building they loved it. Alhamdulillah, many of them still work in software development today, even though they didn't come from that background.

## Install

Download the APK from the download page and open it to install. It needs no internet and no account.
