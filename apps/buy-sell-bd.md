> **An old app, revived as a showcase.** This app was first made around 2019 and its online service has since closed. In 2026 it was rebuilt for modern Android phones with a new design, using sample data instead of a server, so you can see how it worked. Anything you post stays on your phone. Please don't expect it to be actively maintained.

Buy Sell BD is a free buy and sell app for Bangladesh. Selling your old phone or bike shouldn't mean registering and filling in long forms. Post an ad with no account, and buyers near you can find it by division and call you in one tap.

1. **10 categories:** mobiles, electronics, computers, cars, motorbikes, property, home, fashion, hobbies and pets.
2. **Find things near you.** Filter by division (Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi, Rangpur, Barisal, Mymensingh), new or used, and sort by newest or price.
3. **Search** in English or Bangla.
4. **Contact the seller in one tap.** Call, SMS, share or save any ad.
5. **Post an ad with no signup,** with up to 5 photos. Edit it, mark it sold or delete it.
6. **Light and dark mode.** No ads.

Back then, Bangladesh's main marketplace for old stuff was bikroy.com, and it asked for money for some things, which felt unfair to me. So I made this, just to rebel a little. Later Facebook started its own marketplace for free :P I was happy :P

Download the APK from the download page and open it to install. It needs no internet and no account. Sample photos are from Wikimedia Commons; credits are inside the app.
