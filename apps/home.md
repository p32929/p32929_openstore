Home is a clean new tab page extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Instead of a busy page full of news and suggestions, every new tab shows the time, a search box and the sites you actually use, each with its own icon. Your favourite sites are one click away, without digging through bookmarks.

I had already made a homepage like this, FirstPage, because I kept losing important links. Later I knew a bit more about colours and design, so I made this nicer version as a Chrome extension to keep my links close.

1. **Your sites, one click away:** add any site as a tile. Drag tiles to reorder them, or sort by name or by how often you use them.
2. **Search or go:** type a search or a web address right on the new tab.
3. **Save the page you are on:** click the toolbar button to add the current page in one click.
4. **Light, dark or follow your system.**
5. **Starter tiles:** an empty page offers Google, YouTube, Gmail and Wikipedia to add in one click.
6. **Back up your links:** export them to a file and import them on another computer.
7. **Private and fast:** your links stay only in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere, and the page opens instantly.
8. **Free and open source.**

Great for anyone who opens many tabs a day and wants a calm, tidy start page.

Home is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Firefox and other Chromium browsers. It is not on the Chrome Web Store, so you add it yourself in four quick steps:

1. Download `Home.zip` from the download page and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the `Home` folder. Open a new tab and it is there.

On Firefox, open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`, click **Load Temporary Add-on** and pick `manifest.json` inside the `Home` folder. Firefox removes it when it closes, so this is good for trying it out.

To update later, unzip the new version over the same folder and your links stay.
