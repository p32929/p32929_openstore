ListN'Launch is a small app launcher for Android that lets you open any app on your phone, even in ultra or maximum power saving mode, where the phone normally only lets you use a few apps. Add ListN'Launch to the allowed apps once, and from then on every app is one tap away in a fast floating list.

What makes it different: power saving modes on phones like Samsung only let you pick a handful of apps. ListN'Launch takes one of those spots and gives you all the rest, and its built-in guide shows you exactly where that setting is on your phone's brand.

1. **Every app in one grid,** shown in a floating panel over whatever you are doing.
2. **Fast search** that finds apps even with typos and learns which ones you pick.
3. **Favourites and recent apps** above the grid, so the apps you use most are always on top. Recent apps can be turned off.
4. **Long-press any app** to add it to favourites, rename it, hide it from the list or open its app info.
5. **Widgets tab,** to keep home-screen widgets inside the panel too.
6. **Grid size and icon size** settings.
7. **Battery saver guide** for Samsung, Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, Huawei and Honor, with your phone's brand picked for you.

I made it because some phones' ultra battery saver only lets you use a few apps. I use a Galaxy Note 9, and I wanted to open any app even in that mode, so ListN'Launch gives you all your apps from that one spot.

It needs Android 5.0 or newer. How you add it to the power saving mode depends on your phone, and some brands (like Vivo) don't let you add apps there at all.
