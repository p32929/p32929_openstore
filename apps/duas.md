Many of us keep the duas we recite scattered across screenshots, notes and old messages. Finding the right one at the right moment, with its meaning and where it comes from, is harder than it should be.

**Duas keeps them in one calm place.** It starts with a ready-made set, and you add your own by tapping **Add dua** and pasting the Arabic, the meaning and the source. Delete the ones you don't need, and bring them back from Settings if you change your mind.

Not sure of the exact words? Type a topic like *anxiety*, *travelling* or *before sleeping*, and Duas searches four trusted collections at once: **Hisn al-Muslim**, **Dua & Dhikr**, **DeenOne** and **Majmu' al-Adhkar**. Tick the duas you want and add them in one go, each with its source. The Arabic and the references come straight from the books; where a meaning had to be translated by AI, it says so.

Want the meaning in your own language? Tap translate, pick from over 60 languages (or type any other), and compare the translation with the English before you keep it. Translations are clearly marked as made by AI.

Copy or share any dua, choose from eight Arabic fonts, adjust text sizes, and switch between light and dark themes. Your duas stay on your device, with no account needed.

Runs in the browser, on Android, Windows, macOS and iPhone. For **Linux**, older Android phones (armv7) and Chromebooks (x86_64), see all the files on the [releases page](https://github.com/p32929/duas/releases/latest).
