A simple, clean Quran app: free, with no ads and no account.

**Read** every surah ayah by ayah in clear IndoPak Arabic script, with the translation right under each ayah. English, Bengali and Urdu translations come with the app and work offline, and you can download more: over 250 translations in 95 languages.

**Listen** to the whole surah or a single ayah from 42 well-known reciters. Audio keeps playing with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen.

**Memorize** with looped audio: pick a surah and a range of ayat, download the recitation once, and repeat it as many times as you need.

**Find and keep your place:** search surahs by name or number, search words in English or Bengali, bookmark ayat, and pick up where you left off. Share any ayah, change the Arabic and translation text size, and switch between light and dark themes.

Runs in the browser, on Android, Windows, macOS and iPhone. For **Linux**, older Android phones (armv7) and Chromebooks (x86_64), see all the files on the [releases page](https://github.com/p32929/quran_flutter/releases/latest).
